# Brand

The source of truth for how Paperly looks and sounds. If a piece of copy or a design decision contradicts these pages, the pages win (or we update them on purpose).

## Ground rules

1. The mascot is the brand. Use her generously, never distort her.
2. Blue on paper. When in doubt, more white space.
3. We sell calm. Every asset should feel quiet and confident.
