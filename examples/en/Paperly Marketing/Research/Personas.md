# Personas

Based on 32 beta interviews (April and May 2026). Three personas cover 90% of our active users.

## 1. The Burned Migrator

- **Who:** 28 to 45, technical or tech-adjacent, has used 4+ notes apps.
- **Pain:** lost data or formatting in at least one migration. Trusts no cloud.
- **Trigger phrase:** "I just want my files."
- **What convinces them:** opening their existing Obsidian or plain Markdown folder and seeing it work instantly.

## 2. The Calm Organizer

- **Who:** 25 to 40, designer, writer or PM. Aesthetic-driven.
- **Pain:** plain text editors feel ugly; Notion feels heavy and corporate.
- **Trigger phrase:** "I want it to feel like paper."
- **What convinces them:** the editor, the typography, the quiet UI.

## 3. The Privacy Professional

- **Who:** lawyer, therapist, journalist, researcher. Notes are confidential by law or ethics.
- **Pain:** cannot put client notes in a US cloud.
- **Trigger phrase:** "Where is the data stored?"
- **What convinces them:** "Nothing leaves your Mac" plus the fact there is no account at all.

## Anti-persona

Teams needing real-time collaboration. We point them to Notion without irony; see [[Notes App Comparison]].
