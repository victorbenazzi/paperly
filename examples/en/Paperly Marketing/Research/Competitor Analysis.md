# Competitor Analysis

Updated 2026-06-02. Review quarterly.

## Landscape

| App | Model | Price | Their strength | Our wedge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notion | Cloud DB | Freemium | Databases, collab | File ownership, speed |
| Obsidian | Local MD | Free + addons | Plugins, community | Zero-config beauty |
| Apple Notes | Local DB + iCloud | Free | iOS integration | Markdown portability |
| Bear | Local DB + iCloud | Subscription | Polish | Plain files, no export step |

## What we learned from each

- **Notion:** templates sell the dream. Our example vaults play the same role.
- **Obsidian:** the community IS the moat. Start a public roadmap early.
- **Apple Notes:** zero onboarding wins casual users. Our welcome screen must stay two-buttons simple.
- **Bear:** polish is a feature people pay for. Charge for the app, not for sync.

## Positioning sentence

For people who want beautiful notes without giving up their files, Paperly is the Markdown workspace where everything stays in a folder you own.
