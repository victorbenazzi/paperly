# Marketing HQ

Welcome to the Paperly marketing vault. Everything the team ships lives here, as plain Markdown files you could open in any other editor tomorrow.

## Start here

* [[Brand]]: who we are and how we sound
* [[Campaigns]]: what we are shipping and when

## How this vault works

* Every folder can have a page with the same name (a folder note). Click the folder to read its overview.
* Link pages with double brackets, like [[Launch Plan]].
* Nothing here leaves your Mac. No accounts, no sync, no lock-in.

> Tip: press the search shortcut and type a few letters of any page name to jump straight to it.
