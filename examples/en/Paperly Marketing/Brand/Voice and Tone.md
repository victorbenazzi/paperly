# Voice and Tone

Paperly sounds like a calm friend who happens to be very organized.

## Principles

1. **Plain words.** "Your notes are files in a folder", not "a local-first knowledge substrate".
2. **Ownership first.** Every message reinforces that the user owns their data.
3. **Quiet confidence.** No hype, no exclamation marks in product copy.
4. **Short sentences.** If a sentence needs a breath in the middle, split it.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Your notes live in a folder you own." | "Revolutionary AI-powered knowledge OS!!!" |
| "Nothing leaves your Mac." | "Enterprise-grade security solutions." |
| "Point Paperly at a folder you already have." | "Migrate your workspace to our platform." |

## Words we avoid

- "Solution", "platform", "ecosystem" when talking about the app
- "Users" in public copy (say "people" or "you")
- Any em dash. Commas, colons or parentheses instead.
